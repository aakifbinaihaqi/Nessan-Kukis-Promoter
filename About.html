Our Story

Batik Cake was founded by Siti Nurul Jannah Binti Mohd Khalil in 2024 with a simple idea: to create delicious homemade Batik Cake that brings people together.

What started as a small passion for making homemade desserts has grown into a brand dedicated to creating sweet moments for families, friends and loved ones.

We believe that great desserts do not need to be complicated. With quality ingredients, careful preparation and a lot of passion, every Batik Cake is made to be enjoyed from the very first bite to the last.

Our Beginning

Founded in 2024, Batik Cake began with a passion for creating a homemade dessert that is simple, delicious and memorable.

Our Mission

Our mission is to serve delicious Batik Cake made with carefully selected ingredients while providing a warm and enjoyable experience for every customer.

Our Promise

We always aim to maintain great taste, consistent quality and fresh products. Every cake is prepared with care and attention to detail.
